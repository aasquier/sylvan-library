# Gyome, Master Chef — Food — Advanced Primer

**Commander:** Gyome, Master Chef  
**Bracket:** 4  
**Size:** 99 + commander

## Core engine

The floor is Cauldron Familiar plus Witch's Oven: a free recurring drain and, more importantly, a free recurring NONTOKEN creature ETB for Gyome's end-step count. Everything above that is multiplication — Academy Manufactor, Peregrin Took, Bilbo and Parallel Lives all widen the same trigger.

## Lines and sequencing

Sacrifice in response to removal, not before it. Phyrexian Tower and Viscera Seer are free, so a creature about to be exiled should always become value first. Hold Deadly Rollick for the turn an opponent tries to win rather than spending it on a threat you can block.

## Win conditions

Drain loops (Blood Artist / Nadier's Nightblade / Mirkwood Bats), Mazirek or Pippin turning a wide board lethal, Torment of Hailfire off a Food- powered X, or Bolas's Citadel emptying the library.

## Mana base notes

34 lands with 16 basics, safe because Urborg and Yavimaya make every land a dual and four ramp spells specifically want real basics or Overgrown Tomb. A 30-40 sweep at 25,000 games per count does NOT identify a peak: spells deployed through T8 sits between 9.15 and 9.22 across the whole range, which is flat to within noise. The usual "find where deployment plateaus" rule cannot discriminate here, because with 13 ramp pieces and a 3.02 average the deck is limited by cards in hand rather than by mana. What does move is monotonic in both directions — commander-by-T5 climbs 82.7% to 93.3%, wasted mana climbs 10.6 to 13.9, mulligans fall 46% to 28%. 34 is therefore a judgement call inside a broad flat optimum, not a computed optimum, and anything from 33 to 36 is defensible. Note Tier 1 overstates the flood penalty for this deck specifically: it counts unused mana as waste, but Gyome's own {1}-sacrifice, Torment of Hailfire and Bolas's Citadel are real sinks it does not model.

## Politics and table talk

The deck looks like durdle for six turns and then kills the table from a board of Food. Expect to be underestimated early and targeted the moment a drain payoff resolves.

## Failure modes and how to recover

Flooding without Tireless Provisioner, or holding a hand of payoffs with no outlet. The deck has eleven ways to sacrifice for exactly this reason.

## Rules corners worth knowing

Great Arashin City's white Spirit token does not add white to its identity (token colour is not a mana symbol). Jaheira is {2}{G} mono-green; the Background pairing is what makes her read Selesnya. Warren Soultrader costs 1 life per activation, which caps loops that do not gain life back.
