# Gyome, Master Chef — Food — Quick Start

**Commander:** Gyome, Master Chef  
**Bracket:** 4  
**Size:** 99 + commander

## What this deck does

Golgari Food aristocrats. Gyome converts every nontoken creature that enters into Food at end of turn; Food is simultaneously an artifact, a sacrifice cost, a mana source and a lifegain trigger, so the same permanent feeds four different axes. The deck grinds value out of small repeatable loops (Cauldron Familiar recursion, free sacrifice outlets, token multipliers), then converts a wide board into a kill through a drain payoff, Mazirek counters, or Torment of Hailfire.


## The 30-second version

Ramp on 1-3, land Gyome on 4-5, then assemble any outlet plus any payoff. You are rarely trying to combo on a specific turn; you are trying to make every turn produce Food, and Food produce cards.

## Mulligan rule

Keep 2-5 lands. That is the whole rule. Derived from a Tier 1 sweep at 20,000 games/policy: demanding a third mana piece on top of the land count pushes the mulligan rate from 24.6% to 40.2% and buys almost nothing — never-cast-by-T12 moves 0.8% to 0.7% and the median commander turn stays at 4. Requiring three lands is worse still: 55.9% mulligans for a 0.3 point gain. With 13 ramp pieces and 8 of them at MV<=2, two-land hands in this deck are usually fine on their own. Caveat: Tier 1 measures mana and deployment only, not card quality, so a two-lander of pure payoffs is still a mulligan by judgement.

## Turn-by-turn shape

Curve is 9 / 15 / 21 / 13 / 4 / 3 across MV 1-5 then 6+, average nonland MV 3.02. The mass sits at two and three deliberately: Gyome wants bodies entering every turn, not expensive ones.

## Three things that will kill you

(1) Ygra turns your own creatures into artifacts, so Vandalblast and Bane of Progress become one-sided wraths against you. (2) Mirkwood Bats does NOT trigger on a token dying in combat, only on create/sacrifice. (3) Bilbo, Peregrin Took and Academy Manufactor are all replacement effects that each apply once per creation event — they compound but never loop.
