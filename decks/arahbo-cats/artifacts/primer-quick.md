## What this deck does

Selesnya cats-only aggro in two phases. Arahbo has two abilities in two different zones: eminence (+3/+3 to another target Cat at the beginning of combat) works from the command zone and is free from turn one, while the {1}{G}{W} power-doubling-plus-trample is a separate triggered ability that fires whenever another Cat you control ATTACKS, and only functions with Arahbo on the battlefield — it is NOT an eminence ability. So turns 1-4 are a free-anthem aggro deck leaning on double strike and permanent counters, and from turn 5 onward each attacking Cat offers its own {1}{G}{W} trigger, so six mana doubles two attackers, not one. Three kill routes: the doubled trample carrier, a wide alpha strike, or Felidar Sovereign at 40+ life. The last two work without Arahbo on the battlefield, which matters because he costs 7 to recast.


## The 30-second version

Phase 1 (turns 1-4): deploy cheap Cats, attack with one of them under eminence, and convert the temporary +3/+3 into permanent counters with Sovereign Okinec Ahau or Gavony Township. Phase 2 (turn 5+): land Arahbo, then hold three mana every combat to double a carrier with evasion or double strike.

## Mulligan rule

Keep 2-5 lands. Do not demand mana pieces on top of that. From a Tier 1 policy sweep at 20,000 games each: keep 2-5 lands mulligans 21.6% and puts Arahbo down by T5 in 57.2% of games. Adding "lands + ramp(mv<=2) >= 3" nearly doubles the mulligan rate to 41.0% and buys 2.9 points of commander-by-T5 (57.2% to 60.1%) — a bad trade. Demanding three lands is worse: 51.1% mulligans for 64.3%. Median commander turn is 5 under every policy tested, and never-cast-by-T12 never exceeds 3%. Caveat: Tier 1 measures mana and deployment only, not card quality — a two-lander of pure five-drops is still a mulligan by judgement.

## Three things that will kill you

(1) Arahbo's doubling is not eminence — it does nothing from the command zone. (2) Tokens that enter the battlefield already attacking (Leonin Warleader, Nacatl War-Pride) never trigger the doubling: the trigger is "whenever another Cat you control attacks", and a token put onto the battlefield attacking was never declared as an attacker. (3) Neither of Arahbo's abilities can target himself — eminence says "another target Cat" and the doubling says "another Cat you control", so a lone Arahbo swinging is a vanilla 5/5. (4) Cavern of Souls naming Cat makes only colored mana for Cat spells; it produces {C} for the 36 noncreature cards, and it does not help cast True Conviction's {3}{W}{W}{W}.
