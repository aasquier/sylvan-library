## Core engine

Sovereign Okinec Ahau is the card that makes Phase 1 real: his attack trigger converts every temporary pump into permanent +1/+1 counters, including eminence's +3/+3 from the command zone. Order him after Arahbo's doubling and a 1/1 keeps 7 counters. Skullclamp on 1/1 Cat tokens is the card-advantage engine underneath everything.

## Lines and sequencing

Equip Hammer of Nazahn to Arahbo himself — he is the card the deck can least afford to lose. Double strike carriers count the doubled number twice, so Adorned Pouncer under eminence and a doubling is 16 damage. Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist restricting each opponent to one blocker is what a single doubled trampler needs.

## Win conditions

A doubled trample carrier (often through Rogue's Passage), a wide alpha strike behind Jazal Goldmane or Beastmaster Ascension, or Felidar Sovereign at 40+ life fed by the deck's lifelink.

## Mana base notes

36 lands with 16 basics. A 30-40 sweep at 25,000 games per count does NOT identify a peak: spells deployed through T8 sits between 8.42 and 8.48 across the entire range, flat to within noise, so the usual "find where deployment plateaus" rule cannot discriminate. What moves is monotonic in both directions — commander-by-T5 climbs 53.8% to 69.2%, wasted mana through T8 climbs 6.69 to 8.45, mulligans fall 48.5% to 33.2%. 36 is therefore a judgement call inside a broad flat optimum, and 37-38 is defensible. Important caveat specific to this deck: Tier 1 counts unspent mana as waste, but holding {1}{G}{W} open every combat IS the Phase 2 plan, and Gavony Township and Jazal Goldmane are real sinks it does not model — so the flood penalty is overstated here more than for most decks. Colour requirements are lopsided: 48 white pips across 38 cards against 27 white sources (0.56 sources/pip) versus 30 green pips against 26 sources. True Conviction's {3}{W}{W}{W} is the hardest cost in the deck, and Cavern of Souls does not help cast it.

## Politics and table talk

A tribal cats deck reads as durdle until the first doubled double-striker connects. Expect to be left alone through Phase 1 and focused the moment Arahbo resolves.

## Failure modes and how to recover

Arahbo eating removal twice and the commander tax pricing him out, or drawing the top of the curve without the ramp. Both are why Swiftfoot Boots, Teferi's Protection and Akroma's Will are in the list.

## Sideboard / swap philosophy

Command Beacon comes in if Arahbo keeps dying. Doubling Season goes back in if the pod is slow enough that a five-mana do-nothing is affordable.

## Rules corners worth knowing

Ajani, Nacatl Pariah is illegal here — the back face Ajani, Nacatl Avenger carries a colour indicator making him white AND red, so his identity is R/W and outside Selesnya. Eladamri's Call cannot find Arahbo; he is in the command zone, not the library. Loam Lion's Forest condition is almost never live on turn one — Temple Garden is the only land that is both a Forest and a white source.
