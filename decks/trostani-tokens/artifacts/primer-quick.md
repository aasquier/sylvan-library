## What this deck does

Selesnya go-wide tokens built on multiplication rather than addition. Six static token doublers mean two doublers is 4x, three is 8x, four is 16x. The core loop is: land a doubler, generate tokens, Trostani gains life for each one entering, Ocelot Pride sees the lifegain and copies every token that entered this turn, and those copies are themselves doubled by the statics. Two doublers plus Ocelot Pride plus one good token turn is where the 300-token boards come from. Then Craterhoof or Moonshaker Cavalry converts width into a single lethal swing.


## The 30-second version

Ramp on 1-3, deploy a doubler on 4, and live to turn 6. The deck does not need to interact much before then — it needs to not die and to hit land drops. From turn 6 the board doubles faster than anyone can remove it.

## Three things that will kill you

(1) Divine Visitation replaces a populated token's characteristics ENTIRELY, including abilities — so while it is out you cannot populate a large token into a copy of itself, every populate just makes a 4/4 Angel. Still a net win with mostly 1/1s, but the two engines do not stack. (2) Ainok Strike Leader's trigger reads "this creature and/or your commander", and Trostani rarely attacks, so you will mostly trigger it off Ainok itself. (3) No graveyard hate and no fast rebuild after a wrath with all three protection spells spent — accepted trades, not oversights.
