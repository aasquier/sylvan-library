# Trostani, Selesnya's Voice — Tokens — Annotated Decklist

**Commander:** Trostani, Selesnya's Voice  
**Bracket:** 4  
**Size:** 99 + commander

Selesnya go-wide tokens built on multiplication rather than addition. Six static token doublers mean two doublers is 4x, three is 8x, four is 16x. The core loop is: land a doubler, generate tokens, Trostani gains life for each one entering, Ocelot Pride sees the lifegain and copies every token that entered this turn, and those copies are themselves doubled by the statics. Two doublers plus Ocelot Pride plus one good token turn is where the 300-token boards come from. Then Craterhoof or Moonshaker Cavalry converts width into a single lethal swing.


## Command Zone

**Trostani, Selesnya's Voice** — The lifegain trigger is load-bearing, not incidental. Ocelot Pride only fires "if you gained life this turn" and Belladonna Took's first tier is lifegain — Trostani turns every token that enters into a lifegain trigger, so the best engine in the deck is never off. She also gives free repeatable populate with no mana-negative activation and no combat requirement, and her 2/5 body blocks early aggression while you assemble. Ghired is the better populate commander but is Naya, and a third colour for one card is a bad trade in a deck whose mana is already the tightest part of it.
