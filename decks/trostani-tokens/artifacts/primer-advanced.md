**Commander:** Trostani, Selesnya's Voice

## Core engine

Well of Lost Dreams is the single best commander-enabled card here: Trostani gains life for every creature that enters, so the Well converts the entire token engine directly into cards. Cryptolith Rite and Enduring Vitality turn the token board into a second mana base, which is what makes the X-spells and Grand Crescendo lethal.

## Lines and sequencing

Second Harvest at instant speed doubles the whole board on top of the statics — hold it for the turn you are killing, not the turn you are building. Voice of Victory shuts off interaction entirely on your turn, so sequence it before the alpha strike rather than after.

## Win conditions

Craterhoof Behemoth or Moonshaker Cavalry for one lethal swing, Triumph of the Hordes when the table has too much life to punch through, Cathars' Crusade quietly making the board lethal several turns early, or Elspeth, Sun's Champion's ultimate.

## Politics and table talk

You look harmless for five turns and then present lethal from nowhere. The table usually gives you those five turns; the moment a doubler resolves, that stops.

## Failure modes and how to recover

Getting wrathed with protection already spent, or drawing doublers with no generators. The deck has no graveyard recursion to fall back on.

## Sideboard / swap philosophy

Divine Visitation is the clean cut if you would rather run the populate plan uncontaminated — it is the only card actively fighting another engine in the list.

## Rules corners worth knowing

Populate copies a token you control; Divine Visitation's replacement fully overwrites the copy. Minas Tirith and The Shire enter untapped because Trostani is legendary. Ojer Taq triples token creation only while it is a creature, and flips to a land when it dies.
