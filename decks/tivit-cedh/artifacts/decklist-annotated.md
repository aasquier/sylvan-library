# Tivit, Seller of Secrets — Esper cEDH — Annotated Decklist

**Commander:** Tivit, Seller of Secrets  
**Bracket:** 5  
**Size:** 99 + commander

Esper midrange-control at cEDH speed. Fast mana plus free interaction plus hard tutors, protect a window, then win with a compact two-card combo. Tivit himself is a value engine you cast when the coast is clear, not a prerequisite — two of the three win lines do not need him on the battlefield at all. What makes him worth the six mana is that you vote twice on your own council's dilemma, so in a four-player pod every trigger is exactly five artifacts and opponents choose only the flavour, never the count.


## Command Zone

**Tivit, Seller of Secrets** — Two clauses drive everything. You vote an additional time, so a three-opponent pod means five votes and five artifacts per trigger that opponents cannot deny you. And the trigger is ETB OR combat damage, not "attacks" — which is why a single blocker or a fog turns off the Time Sieve loop and why combat has to be planned around it.
