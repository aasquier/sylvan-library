# Tivit, Seller of Secrets — Esper cEDH — Quick Start

**Commander:** Tivit, Seller of Secrets  
**Bracket:** 5  
**Size:** 99 + commander

## What this deck does

Esper midrange-control at cEDH speed. Fast mana plus free interaction plus hard tutors, protect a window, then win with a compact two-card combo. Tivit himself is a value engine you cast when the coast is clear, not a prerequisite — two of the three win lines do not need him on the battlefield at all. What makes him worth the six mana is that you vote twice on your own council's dilemma, so in a four-player pod every trigger is exactly five artifacts and opponents choose only the flavour, never the count.


## The 30-second version

Turns 1-2 land, rock, engine — Rhystic Study and especially Mystic Remora down as early as possible, since Remora's value curve collapses after turn four. Turn 3-4 either deploy Tivit with a counter held up or hold everything and answer the pod's fastest deck. Then win off Oracle, the Sieve loop, or the Kitten line.

## Mulligan rule

Keep hands that do TWO of these three: produce fast mana, interact for free, or dig. Two lands + a rock + a tutor is a keep; three lands with no acceleration or interaction is not — ship it, this deck functions on five. Auto-keeps: Gemstone Caverns on the draw, Ancient Tomb plus two rocks, or Wishclaw plus protection. You do not need Tivit in your opener; you need mana and answers. Note this policy is hand-quality based and is NOT what Tier 1's land-count mulligan models — do not quote sim mulligan rates for this deck as if they described this rule.

## Three things that will kill you

(1) The Time Sieve loop needs FIVE artifacts per iteration and you only get five votes with three living opponents — kill someone and the loop breaks at four unless Lotho, Smothering Tithe, an Urza's Saga Construct or a leftover Clue bridges the gap. Sequence eliminations LAST. (2) Grafdigger's Cage turns off your own Sevinne's Reclamation flashback. (3) Cursed Totem shuts off Ranger-Captain's own sacrifice ability, though it does not touch Tivit, Time Sieve, Clues, Treasures or the Oracle trigger.
