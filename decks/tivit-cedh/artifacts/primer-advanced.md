**Commander:** Tivit, Seller of Secrets  
**Bracket:** 5

## Core engine

Tainted Pact works only because the deck runs exactly one copy of each basic name — one Island, one Swamp, no duplicate names anywhere — so the library exiles fully. Four free counters (Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation) is the number that lets you tap out for Tivit and still hold the table.

## Lines and sequencing

Crack Wishclaw Talisman only when you can win on the spot, or when the next player is dead before untapping. Hold Swan Song and An Offer for the win attempt, not for a Rhystic Study. Ward {3} means opponents need real mana to kill Tivit, so often you simply let a removal spell resolve and rebuild rather than spend a free counter. Infinite turns is a soft lock, not a kill — use the turns to crack Clues, dig, and assemble Oracle.

## Win conditions

(1) Thassa's Oracle plus Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact — two cards, four mana, instant speed, and the default plan. (2) Time Sieve plus Tivit for infinite turns. (3) Displacer Kitten plus Teferi, Time Raveler plus any mana-positive artifact to draw the deck, then Oracle; Teferi's static locks opponents to sorcery speed while you go off.

## Mana base notes

30 lands, deliberately low, leaning on 13 fast mana pieces. All three Talismans are here because Tivit costs {3}{W}{U}{B} — coloured sources, not raw mana, are the bottleneck. One Island and one Swamp only, both to stay fetchable under Blood Moon effects and to keep Tainted Pact live. Reserved List is ALLOWED here but only three cards use it (Tundra, Underground Sea, Scrubland); the surveil-land substitutions cost little.

## Politics and table talk

Opponents will sometimes vote to deny you Treasures; it does not matter, every vote is still an artifact and Time Sieve does not care which kind. Announce both your votes at once — you vote first, so everyone else has full information. That is the tradeoff for voting twice.

## Failure modes and how to recover

Casting Tivit into an open blue player with nothing held back is how this deck loses. Consultation lines lose to a single Flusterstorm; do not jam without cover unless the table is tapped out.

## Sideboard / swap philosophy

Ten flex slots, each naming what it replaces. Academy Manufactor turns each trigger into 15 artifacts but is dead on its own. The Underworld Breach package adds a third win but wants a more storm-focused shell.

## Rules corners worth knowing

Voice of Victory is legal in Esper: its Warrior tokens are red, but the card has no red mana symbols, so its colour identity is white only. Mana Crypt and Jeweled Lotus are banned as of the September 2024 update — do not go looking for them. Imposter Mech copies a creature as a NONCREATURE Vehicle, so it dodges creature removal and wraths and still counts for Mox Opal and Time Sieve.
