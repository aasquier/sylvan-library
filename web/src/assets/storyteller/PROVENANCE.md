## storyteller-camp-loop.webm / .mp4 / storyteller-camp-still.webp / storyteller-camp-tone.m4a

- **Source**: generated by Aaron on his own machine with Seedance, 2026-10-03, from a scene brief Claude wrote in-session for the storyteller's room ("Trade stories"): a night campfire in a snowy northern clearing, photographed from a seat on the ground on one side of the fire -- birch logs burning steady in a ring of stones, an empty log bench across the fire with a fur cloak thrown over it and a drinking horn set down, a pine treeline, stars, and a green and violet aurora. Birgi is Kaldheim's, so the room got the sky. There is no upstream file and no third party to ask: the brief was ours, the render is Aaron's, and nothing was fetched.
- **Licence**: Aaron's, outright. The séance room's check (2026-08-18) is the argument -- the platform behind Seedance asserts no ownership over generated output and requires no attribution, and redistribution is unrestricted, which is what this repo's gate actually asks. **The terms were not re-read for this clip**; the séance file says a future clip should re-check rather than inherit, and that is owed here and recorded as owed.

  **The content judgement for this clip**: fire, snow, logs, fur, a horn, pines, stars and an aurora -- no people, no marks, no text, nothing recognisably anyone's. Wizards' marks appear nowhere in it.
- **Delivered**: 1280x720, 15.07 s, 361 frames at 24 fps, H.264 with an AAC stereo track at 32 kHz.
- **Transformations** (ffmpeg 7.1 via the toolbox venv's `imageio-ffmpeg`, and the numpy seam script beside the prompts in the session's scratchpad; **not** through `animist`):
  - The loop is frames 0-293 (12.21 s). A fire is the hardest thing to dissolve across, so the cut was searched rather than assumed: on a 320x180 grey proxy, the pair of frames at least 12 s apart that differ least is 0 and 293 at a mean 6.11/255 (the whole clip end-to-end is 8.04; the best 10 s cut is 5.31, the best 6 s cut 4.53). Twelve seconds was chosen over six because a loop twice as long repeats half as often and the extra 1.6 of difference is spent inside a flame, where the dissolve reads as the flame moving.
  - Made seamless the séance way: the last 12 frames cross-dissolved into the first 12 (`blend=all_expr='A*(1-N/12)+B*(N/12)'`), leaving 281 frames (11.71 s).
  - Encoded twice from a lossless intermediate: VP9 `-crf 36 -b:v 0` (1.28 MB) and H.264 `-crf 27 -preset slow -profile:v high` (1.33 MB), both `yuv420p`, the mp4 with `+faststart`. Video only. Three times the séance clip's weight for half again its length -- snow, stars, sparks and an aurora are all noise to an encoder -- and accepted as the cost of the room; revisit the CRF if the bundle budget ever minds.
  - `storyteller-camp-still.webp` is frame 150 at quality 80 -- the reduced-motion floor and the ambience-off picture.
  - `storyteller-camp-tone.m4a` is the clip's whole track, looped in numpy: the last 0.5 s equal-power cross-faded into the first 0.5 s; 14.57 s, AAC 96 kb/s. Measured RMS -37.0 dBFS, peak -1.6 dBFS -- the peak is a crackle. Plays only behind the table-sound switch (`web/src/components/roomtone.tsx`).
- **Measured** (ITU-R 601 luma of the delivered frames): mean 40.8, drifting from 42.6 to 39.3 over the clip as the aurora moves. Just under the house's dark cluster (mean 48-64); the firelight carries the frame.
