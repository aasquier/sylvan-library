# agatha assets -- provenance

The rule here is the tarot deck's (`assets/tarot/PROVENANCE.md`): every transformation applied is written down, so the derivation is reproducible from the delivered file. And the exception is the séance room's (`web/src/assets/seance/PROVENANCE.md`, its last section): these files were authored on Aaron's own machine and not fetched through the animist gate, so the record is hand-written and says so. The accounting sweep (`go/cmd/mtglab/mediaprovenance_test.go`) reads this file by stem.

## What this directory used to hold

From 2026-09-12 to 2026-10-03 the hut's plate was **John William Waterhouse, *The Magic Circle*, 1886, Tate, London** -- a public-domain oil fetched through the animist gate from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to its lower third (`frac_box=[0.0, 0.5574, 1.0, 0.9398]`) and resized to 1280 wide, with a stylesheet dusk holding its daylight back and a drawn fire breathing under the pot. The recipe (`agatha-hut-plate.recipe.yaml`) and the plate are deleted with this record rather than left beside it, because a recipe whose output is gone fails `animist verify`, and the credit the room printed for the painting went with the painting: a credit for a picture that is not in the room would be the one lie in it. The painting was the right floor for a room that had no footage; the footage was always the plan (`the-interview-rooms-overhaul` memory, "the Seedance hut render (documented drop-in)"), and the drop-in happened the way the component said it would -- `webmSrc`/`mp4Src` added to the plate and the four mouth percentages re-measured.

## agatha-hut-loop.webm / .mp4 / agatha-hut-still.webp / agatha-hut-tone.m4a, and agatha-brew-base / agatha-brew-heat / agatha-brew-binding (.webm / .mp4 / .m4a each)

- **Source**: one render, generated by Aaron on his own machine with Seedance, 2026-10-03, from a scene brief Claude wrote in-session: the inside of a witch's hut at night from the doorway, a black iron cauldron dead centre over a low wood fire, a dark umber brew simmering, herbs from the beams, jars, a wicker cage, a ladle, a window on a green-tinged wood -- and, because credits were short, all three reactions in the same fifteen seconds: rose petals and berries (The Base), a dark powder and a chilli (The Heat), roots and leaves (The Binding), each going into the pot and the pot answering. Seven files are cut from that one clip. There is no upstream file and no third party to ask: the brief was ours, the render is Aaron's, and nothing was fetched.
- **Licence**: Aaron's, outright. The séance room's check (2026-08-18) is the argument -- the platform behind Seedance asserts no ownership over generated output and requires no attribution, and redistribution is unrestricted, which is what this repo's gate actually asks. **The terms were not re-read for this clip**; the séance file says a future clip should re-check rather than inherit, and that is owed here and recorded as owed.

  **The content judgement for this clip**: iron, embers, steam, jars, dried herbs, a cage, a ladle, petals, a chilli, roots -- no people, no hands (checked frame by frame at 2 fps: the ingredients enter from above the frame and nothing holds them), no marks, no text, nothing recognisably anyone's. Wizards' marks appear nowhere in it.
- **Delivered**: 1280x720, 15.07 s, 361 frames at 24 fps, H.264 with an AAC stereo track at 32 kHz. The brief asked for five quiet seconds first; the render put petals in the air at frame 0 and the pot is quiet only from about 13.4 s. The idle loop is cut from that quiet, and the reactions from the events, as follows.
- **Transformations** (ffmpeg 7.1 via the toolbox venv's `imageio-ffmpeg`, and the numpy scripts beside the prompts in the session's scratchpad; **not** through `animist`):
  - **The idle loop** is frames 322-361: the brew back to umber (mean RGB over the mouth 66/55/49 by frame 331, against 61/51/46 before any ingredient), steam thin, frame-to-frame change 0.3-0.4/255. Thirty-nine frames is 1.6 s, too short to loop, so it is **slowed to half speed** with motion-compensated interpolation (`minterpolate=fps=48:mi_mode=mci:mc_mode=aobmc:vsbmc=1`, then `setpts=2*PTS`) to 78 frames, and made seamless the séance way (the last 12 frames cross-dissolved into the first 12), leaving 66 frames -- 2.75 s of steam and slow bubbles.
  - **The reactions** are plain trims, no seam, since each is a one-shot that fades back to the loop: The Base is frames 0-66 (petals mid-air at frame 0, the splash, the rose steam and the gold bloom; it ends with the brew still pink, and the room's own fade-out is what returns it to umber); The Heat is frames 76-180 (the chilli entering, the flare peaking at frame 96, embers again by 170); The Binding is frames 196-330 (the roots entering at about 200, the green climbing the walls, umber again by 325). Each clip's landing beat -- the millisecond the thing meets the surface -- is written in `web/src/components/cauldron.tsx` (`REACTIONS[...].landAt`: 500, 850 and 900 ms), read off these frames.
  - All seven videos encoded twice: VP9 `-crf 36 -b:v 0` and H.264 `-crf 27 -preset slow -profile:v high`, both `yuv420p`, the mp4s with `+faststart`, video only. Sizes: loop 112 KB / 154 KB; base 249 KB / 302 KB; heat 687 KB / 715 KB (a flare is noise); binding 465 KB / 574 KB.
  - `agatha-hut-still.webp` is frame 345 at quality 80 -- the reduced-motion floor, the ambience-off picture, and **the frame the four mouth percentages were measured on**.
  - `agatha-hut-tone.m4a` is the four event-free stretches of the clip's audio played forwards and in clip order — 6.5-8.5 s, 9.0-9.6 s, 13.0-15.07 s and 0.0-0.75 s (found by RMS and spectral centroid per quarter second: the splashes, the flare and the hisses all stand out on both) — joined with 0.5 s equal-power cross-fades and looped in numpy with the last 0.7 s faded into the first 0.7 s; 3.22 s, AAC 96 kb/s, RMS -43.5 dBFS. Three earlier cuts are recorded because each taught something: 2.5 s from two stretches with 0.5 s fades was audibly a loop; 7.5 s of six pieces, two of them played backwards, with 0.3 s joins jittered at the joins to Aaron's ear; and two stretches with a 0.9 s seam came out 2.5 s again, because a loop's seam fade is subtracted from its length. A simmer wants few seams and slow ones, and the length it can afford after that. The three `agatha-brew-*.m4a` are each reaction's own stretch of the track, with a 0.1 s fade in and a 0.4 s fade out. All play only behind the table-sound switch (`web/src/components/roomtone.tsx`, `tablesounds.clip`).

## The hotspot, re-measured

The cauldron's mouth, as percentages of the 1280x720 frame -- the same contract as before (`--pot-x/-y/-d/-dy` in `web/src/index.css`), measured on `agatha-hut-still.webp`:

```css
--pot-x: 49.9%;   /* centre of the mouth, across the frame */
--pot-y: 54.9%;   /* centre of the mouth, down the frame */
--pot-d: 27.2%;   /* the mouth's MAJOR axis, over the frame's width */
--pot-dy: 5.3%;   /* the mouth's MINOR axis, over the frame's height */
```

Read off the **inner edge of the rim**, which is the opening anything landing in the brew wants: x 36.3% to 63.5%, y 52.2% to 57.5%. The rim's own outer bounding box (bright grey, low saturation, in the band y 46-62%) is x 29.8-66.2%, y 49.3-61.8% -- wider, because the lip is thick and seen from slightly above. Two passes: a grid overlay at 5% on a 2x crop of the pot (`witch-pot-zoom.png` in the scratchpad) to read the inner edge by eye, and the threshold bounding boxes to confirm the eye was inside the lip. The ember bed's centroid, by the warmth-times-luma statistic restricted to the lower half of the frame, is at (53.0%, 83.9%); no layer reads it any more (the drawn fire went with the painting), recorded so the next measurement has a prior.

**Measured** (ITU-R 601 luma of frame 345): mean 19.6, p50 11.7, p90 47.7, p99 110.3. Well under the house's dark cluster (mean 48-64); it is a hut at night lit by embers, and Aaron's eye accepted it before any number was taken. Across the clip the mean swings from 19 (quiet) to 66 (the flare at 4 s), which is the reactions doing what they are for.

**Re-measure all four against the still of any footage that replaces this one, and nothing else.** Every effect in the room is anchored to them, so the drawn fall (for the readers who get the still rather than the film) lands in the pot at every window width, and the folded strip is the same composition cropped to a band around the same four numbers rather than a second measurement.
