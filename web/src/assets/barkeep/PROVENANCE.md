## barkeep-room-loop.webm / .mp4 / barkeep-room-still.webp / barkeep-room-tone.m4a

- **Source**: generated by Aaron on his own machine with Seedance, 2026-10-03, from a scene brief Claude wrote in-session for the barkeep's room ("Pull up a stool"): a medieval tavern at the quiet hour before the rush, photographed from a stool at the bar -- a pewter tankard, a clear glass and a folded polishing cloth on worn oak, a candle in a bottle, shelves of dark bottles and clay jugs lit from below, casks with brass taps, pewter mugs on hooks, a stone hearth, a leaded window on blue dusk and rain. There is no upstream file and no third party to ask: the brief was ours, the render is Aaron's, and nothing was fetched.
- **Licence**: Aaron's, outright. The séance room's check (2026-08-18) is the argument -- the platform behind Seedance asserts no ownership over generated output and requires no attribution, and redistribution is unrestricted, which is what this repo's gate actually asks. **The terms were not re-read for this clip**; the séance file says a future clip should re-check rather than inherit, and that is owed here and recorded as owed.

  **The content judgement for this clip**: oak, pewter, glass, candles, a hearth, bottles whose paper labels are illegible at the delivered resolution -- no people, no marks, no readable text, nothing recognisably anyone's. Wizards' marks appear nowhere in it.
- **Delivered**: 1280x720, 15.07 s, 361 frames at 24 fps, H.264 with an AAC stereo track at 32 kHz.
- **Transformations** (ffmpeg 7.1 via the toolbox venv's `imageio-ffmpeg`, and the numpy seam script beside the prompts in the session's scratchpad; **not** through `animist`):
  - The loop is the whole clip. On a 320x180 grey proxy the first and last frames differ by a mean 1.29/255 -- a quarter of the séance clip's 4.23 -- so no sub-range was cut.
  - Made seamless the séance way: the last 12 frames cross-dissolved into the first 12 (`blend=all_expr='A*(1-N/12)+B*(N/12)'`), leaving the 349 frames (14.54 s) that survive.
  - Encoded twice from a lossless intermediate: VP9 `-crf 36 -b:v 0` (403 KB) and H.264 `-crf 27 -preset slow -profile:v high` (584 KB), both `yuv420p`, the mp4 with `+faststart`. Video only.
  - `barkeep-room-still.webp` is frame 100 at quality 80 -- the reduced-motion floor and the ambience-off picture.
  - `barkeep-room-tone.m4a` is the clip's whole track, looped in numpy: the last 0.5 s equal-power cross-faded into the first 0.5 s, so the seam is silent about itself; 14.57 s, AAC 96 kb/s. Measured RMS -38.5 dBFS, peak -14.2 dBFS -- a quiet bed, which is what it is for. Plays only behind the table-sound switch (`components/roomtone.tsx`).
- **Measured** (ITU-R 601 luma of the delivered frames): mean 25.8, steady to within 0.4 across the clip. Under the house's dark cluster (mean 48-64), and accepted on Aaron's eye: it is a tavern at the quiet hour lit by candles, and the words in the room sit on their own surfaces.
