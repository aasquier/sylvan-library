# seance assets -- provenance

The rule here is the tarot deck's (`src/mtglab/assets/tarot/PROVENANCE.md`): nothing ships in this directory whose licence was not checked per file, and every transformation applied is written down so the derivation is reproducible from the source.

<!-- animist:begin crystal -->
## seance-candles.webp

- **Source**: "Burning candles, wax running (public domain, CC0)", <https://www.rawpixel.com/image/5963706/free-public-domain-cc0-photo>, found via Openverse with a license=cc0 filter, searching `dripping wax candle`.
- **Licence**: cc0. Confirmed through the Openverse record API at fetch time (2026-08-17).
- **Transformations** (Pillow, scripted -- `mtglab animist build crystal.recipe.yaml`):
  - `crop`: frac_box=[0.0, 0.14, 0.8, 0.74].
  - `duotone`: shadow='#000000', mid='#8a6b3e', light='#fff4cf'.
  - `resize`: width=780.
  - Encoded WEBP, quality 84.

Why committed rather than hotlinked: The photograph is CC0, so committing the derived asset is clean, and it is the ivy's argument again: a decoration that breaks when a third-party CDN moves is a decoration the app does not own. The two loops are generated by this pipeline, so committing them is the only way they exist at all -- no stock smoke, nothing hotlinked, just weather of our own making.

## seance-parchment.webp

- **Source**: "Folio from a Qur'an Manuscript", <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/448369>, found via the Met Open Access API, searching `parchment` after Wikimedia's best vellum plates all turned out to be CC BY -- an attribution obligation a decoration must not carry (the gate refuses it, and the gate is right)
.
- **Licence**: CC0. Confirmed through met at fetch time (2026-08-17).
- **Transformations** (Pillow, scripted -- `mtglab animist build parchment.recipe.yaml`):
  - `crop`: frac_box=[0.0, 0.7132, 0.2204, 0.9327].
  - `levels`: out_black='#b1945f'.
  - Encoded WEBP, quality 84.

Why committed rather than hotlinked: The folio is CC0 through the museum's own isPublicDomain flag, so the derived tile is clean to commit, and the ivy's argument holds one more time: the paper under the reader's own words must not break when a third-party CDN moves. Four kilobytes of webp is cheaper than the gradient stack it replaces.
