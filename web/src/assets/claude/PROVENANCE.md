# claude assets -- provenance

The rule here is the tarot deck's (`src/mtglab/assets/tarot/PROVENANCE.md`):
every transformation applied is written down, so the derivation is
reproducible from the source. Like the séance room, everything in this
directory is **authored** rather than fetched -- there is no upstream file,
no provider API to ask, and no recipe through the animist gate. The honest
confirmation is this file, and the gap it sits in is the known one: the
pipeline still has no `authored` source kind
(`web/src/assets/seance/PROVENANCE.md` states it at length).

## claude-library-loop.webm / .mp4 / claude-library-still.webp

- **Source**: generated by Aaron on his own machine with Seedance, 2026-08-18,
  from a scene brief Claude wrote in-session for the About Claude page's
  masthead (a moonlit library aisle holding a warm ringed light -- the page's
  own mark, made real). There is no upstream file and no third party to ask:
  the brief was ours, the render is Aaron's, and nothing was fetched.
- **Licence**: Aaron's, outright. Checked 2026-08-18 (same check as the
  séance room's, made the same day): the platform behind Seedance (Volcengine
  Ark) asserts no intellectual-property ownership over generated output,
  grants commercial use, and requires no attribution to the model, the
  platform or ByteDance. **Redistribution is unrestricted, and that -- not
  the non-commercial question -- is what this repo's gate actually asks**,
  since committing an asset to a public repository *is* redistribution.

  **The residual risk is not the licence** -- a generative model's exposure
  attaches to the *output*: whether this clip contains somebody else's
  protected material. **The judgement for this clip**: a full moon, ivy- and
  moss-grown bookshelves with illegible spines, an arched window, indigo
  night -- no characters, no marks, no legible text, nothing recognisably
  anyone's. Wizards' marks appear nowhere in it. A future clip is its own
  judgement; the studio-pressure situation around video models was moving
  when this was checked, so re-check rather than inherit this paragraph.
- **Transformations** (ffmpeg 7.1 via imageio-ffmpeg, and **not** through
  `mtglab animist`):
  - The delivered clip is 1280x720, 10.04s, 241 frames at 24fps, video only
    (no audio track was delivered).
  - Made seamless by **ping-pong** rather than the séance room's
    cross-dissolve, and the choice was measured, not stylistic: the clip
    carries a slow push-in, so first-versus-last mean absolute difference is
    19.86/255 (the séance's was 4.23) against an adjacent-frame noise floor
    of 2.06 -- a short dissolve would visibly pop the moon's scale. Instead
    the clip plays forward then reversed
    (`split[a][b];[b]reverse,trim=start_frame=1:end_frame=240[r];[a][r]concat`,
    the trim dropping the two frames the mirror would duplicate), giving 480
    frames / 19.96s whose loop seam measures 3.32/255 -- at the noise floor.
    The reversed half reads as the camera breathing back out of the aisle.
  - The ping-pong master was cut at H.264 crf 18 (an intermediate, not
    shipped), then encoded twice: VP9 `-crf 40 -b:v 0 -row-mt 1` (766 KB)
    and H.264 `-crf 28 -preset slow -profile:v high` (1.31 MB), both
    `yuv420p`, mp4 with `+faststart`.
  - `claude-library-still.webp` is frame 120 of the master at quality 80
    (45 KB) -- the poster and the reduced-motion floor, since the masthead
    with motion unwelcome must still be a room and not a hole.
