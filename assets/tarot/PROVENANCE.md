# Where these 78 pictures came from, and why they are allowed to be here

**Edition: the original "Roses & Lilies" Rider–Waite–Smith deck, published by
the Rider Company, London, December 1909.** Illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith
(1878–1951) to Arthur Edward Waite's (1857–1942) direction.

Downloaded 2026-08-13 from Wikimedia Commons,
[Category:Rider-Waite tarot deck (Roses & Lilies)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)).

## The licence

Public domain in both the United States and the United Kingdom.

- **United States:** published 1909, which is before the 1929 cutoff, so the
  work is in the public domain there outright.
- **United Kingdom** (the source country): the term is life of the author plus
  70 years. Sources disagree about *which* author's death starts the clock —
  Waite's (1942) or Smith's (1951) — and it does not matter here, because both
  candidates expired before this was written. 1951 + 70 = 2021.

All 78 files carried exactly one licence tag on Commons: **`Public domain`**.
That was checked per file through the Commons API rather than read off the
category page, and the check is reproducible — the category listing reports
`LicenseShortName` for every member.

## The trap this deck is famous for

**The version almost everybody pictures is still in copyright.** US Games
Systems published their edition in 1971 and hold rights to it: that is the
bright, flat-coloured, white-bordered deck sold in every bookshop. Scans of it
are not usable here no matter how often they are described online as "the
public domain tarot".

Two things guard against one slipping in:

1. **Every upstream filename carries `RWS1909`.** The fetch script skips
   anything without that prefix and says so — the two files it skipped are the
   category's title images, not cards.
2. **The 1909 printing looks different, and obviously so.** Cream, aged stock
   with visible paper grain; muted, uneven colour; a thin black keyline; and
   the card title set in serif capitals with a trailing full stop —
   `THE STAR .` The 1971 deck has none of those.

If these files are ever replaced or re-fetched, check both.

## What was done to them

Resized to 400px wide and converted to WebP at quality 75 — 22.1 MB of source
JPEG down to 4.6 MB. No cropping, no colour correction, no retouching. 400px
was chosen at the knee of the size curve: 560px cost 11.0 MB and 340px saved
only 1.5 MB more while starting to soften the linework.
